buzzdetect live!

Runs a compressed version of buzzdetect's model_general_v3 on audio. All processing happens locally on your browser; audio isn't uploaded.

Getting audio in

  • You can ether open an existing audio file (whatever formats your browser supports), or you can record from your microphone.
  • Live recording only retains up to five minutes of audio. You can watch the analysis stream in during recording; afterwards, you can browse it like a normal file.

Navigation

  • Click on the spectrogram (top panel) to seek the playhead to that position.
  • Click and drag to create a selection, bounding playback to the selected range.
  • Click on the buzzdtect results (bottom panel) to select that frame of buzzdetect results.
  • You can pan by scrolling (swipe on mobile) and zoom by shift-scrolling (pinch-zoom on mobile).
  • Press the spacebar to play/pause. Step the playhead with the arrow keys (hold shift for larger steps).
  • You can enter a desired time in the running seconds at the bottom, in the navigation toolbar. Use raw seconds or MM:SS.SS.

Panels

  • The top panel is the spectrogram, a visual representation of the audio.
  • The vertical axis is frequency, in Hz. The horizontal axis is time. The color is amplitude (volume).
  • Some files/environments are noisier than others; use the slider to the right of the spectrogram to dial in the color scale.
  • The bottom panel shows the buzzdetect results. The vertical axis is activation, a somewhat abstract representation of the model's confidence that the corresponding event is present in the frame. Each frame is a 0.96s segment of audio that the model analyzes and makes predictions for.
  • The Neurons button lets you pick what neurons you want to plot. Note! We only tune buzzdetect for performance on the ins_buzz neuron, but you may find the others informative. You can set a detection threshold for each - a level above which you want to consider the frame positive for the event in question. Our baseline threshold for the ins_buzz neuron is -1.2
