buzzdetect live

Runs buzzdetect's model_general_v3 on audio, entirely in this tab. Nothing is uploaded and nothing leaves the machine.

Getting audio in

  • Open an audio file, or record from the microphone.
  • Recording keeps the last five minutes; stopping turns it into an ordinary file.

Moving around

  • Click to seek. Hold and drag to select a range; press Escape to clear it.
  • Click a bar in the results to select exactly the audio that frame scored.
  • Scroll or pinch to zoom, shift-scroll or swipe sideways to pan.
  • Space plays and pauses; the arrow keys step, and shift makes them step further.
  • Type a position into the time readout, as seconds or MM:SS.SS.

The display

  • The spectrogram's vertical axis is mel-spaced, matching what the model sees.
  • The slider beside it sets the contrast window; the Hz boxes set the range shown.
  • Results are the model's raw activation for ins_buzz — unbounded model units, not probabilities. The threshold is in those same units.
